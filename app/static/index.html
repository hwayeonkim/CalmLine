CalmLine

Shielding public-service call agents from verbal abuse — in real time

The industry trains the people being abused. CalmLine reduces the exposure.

Caller audio reaches the agent a couple of seconds late — like a broadcast delay. In that window CalmLine transcribes the call, mutes abusive words, turns shouting down to conversational loudness, and hands the agent a calm card with every fact preserved. Threats raise a supervisor alert. The original is sealed as evidence; the agent stays in control.
